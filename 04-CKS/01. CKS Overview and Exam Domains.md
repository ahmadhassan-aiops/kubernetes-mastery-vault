# 01. CKS Overview and Exam Domains

## What Is the CKS?

The **Certified Kubernetes Security Specialist (CKS)** shows that you have the skills to secure container-based applications and Kubernetes platforms during **build, deployment and runtime**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prerequisite** | An **active CKA** certification. You can buy the CKS before passing the CKA, but you can't schedule it until you have the CKA, and the CKA must still be valid on your exam (and retake) date. |
| **Format** | Performance-based: you solve real tasks in a simulated environment, not multiple-choice questions |
| **Who it's for** | Accomplished Kubernetes practitioners who secure clusters professionally |

## Security Works in Layers

Security is applied at several levels: **cloud**, **cluster**, **container** and **code** (the "4Cs", covered in [KCSA topic 01](../05-KCSA/01.%20The%204Cs%20of%20Cloud%20Native%20Security.md)). The CKS focuses mostly on the cluster and container layers.
